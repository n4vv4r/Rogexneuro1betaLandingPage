Safety

Verify before you flash

Every image ships with a SHA256 checksum published alongside its release on GitHub Releases and SourceForge. Compare your local digest:

sha256sum echos-1.0-*-x86_64.iso

If a checksum does not match the published value, do not boot the image and report it via the channels listed in the FAQ.
