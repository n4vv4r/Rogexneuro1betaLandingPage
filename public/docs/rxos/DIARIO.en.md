## We discovered that…

The industry treats intelligence as **more parameters and more watts**. A GPU campus bets the next syllable. If it does not know an asteroid’s mass, **it invents it** and it sounds fine.

That does not fit on a laptop. It does not fit on a USB for someone who should not rent the cloud. It does not fit in a lab that measures joules and refuses to fake them.

Three things you can touch:

1. A thought does not need a hangar. **Sixteen bytes** name the act (who, verb, object, when, and six axes).
2. A fact does not live in the weights. It lives on a **card**. No extract: the honest answer is *I don’t know*.
3. Spanish is a **mask**. The voltage is the protocol.

This is not a complaint. It is the problem we chose to solve.

## We invented… to solve this problem

**RogexWSP** — a 16-byte thought. `_Static_assert`. The size of a 1999 SMS.

**NAVI 10 Echo** — an SNN mind that already runs on the host. CAM of 4096 slots, Heap-0, Hamming in one `popcount`. Questions, jokes, math, repo code, your IP. No card: **UNKNOWN**. That is a virtue.

**rxOS** — a unikernel, not trimmed Linux. Today you can boot **9 SMOKE** (QEMU, VirtualBox, USB). **rxOS 10** is the next artifact: Echo travels *inside* the system.

We do not invent joules for a chip that is not on the table. Akida remains a destination, not an ad.

## We are looking for…

People who want to **run it**, not admire it from afar.

Silicon partners — BrainChip or another NPU — **without pretending** a board is already here.

Licenses that fund access: those who can pay OEM **pay**; students and makers **get in**.

A plaza, not a cult. Knights Computer Club stays the club. The lab pushes. The lodge, if it comes, comes *after* the 10/10 crossing.

## Where we are going

One sheet. Time on the left; the faces of the company on the right. No invented dates.

The order we can keep:

1. **Now** — NAVI 10 on the host. ISO 9 downloads.
